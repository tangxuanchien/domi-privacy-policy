Privacy Policy

Domi: Photobooth Camera · com.txclabs.photobooth
Last updated: 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Domi: Photobooth Camera ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we process

  • Camera & photos. The App uses your device camera to take photos and lets you decorate them with frames, stickers, filters and fonts. Photos are processed on your device and are saved to your device (Pictures/Domi Photobooth) only when you tap Save. We do not upload your photos to our servers. You can delete saved photos at any time from the Library tab or your gallery.
  • Photos from your gallery. If you choose to use existing photos, the App uses the Android photo picker and only receives the photos you select. If you tap Share, the finished photo is handed to the app you choose in Android's share sheet.
  • Downloaded content. Frames, stickers, fonts and the catalog that lists them are downloaded from Cloudinary (cloudinary.com), a third-party image hosting and delivery service, so content can be updated without an app update. Like any web request, this makes your IP address and basic request details (such as the app's user agent) visible to Cloudinary. We do not send it any personal data about you or your photos.
  • Usage & diagnostics. We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage. This may include a device identifier, device model, OS version, language, app version and in-app events. It is used only in aggregate to improve the App.
  • Stored on your device. Settings such as your language choice and a cached copy of the catalog are kept locally on your device and are not sent to us.

2. What we do NOT do

  • We do not require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not collect your contacts, location or browsing history.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-party services

The App relies on the following services. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies:

  • Cloudinary (content delivery): cloudinary.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Analytics (usage statistics): policies.google.com/privacy

4. Permissions

  • Camera — to capture photos in the photobooth.
  • Internet — to download the frame / sticker / font catalog.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only) — to save finished photos to your gallery. Newer Android versions do not need this permission.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

6. Data retention

Photos you save stay on your device until you delete them (in the Library tab or in your gallery). Aggregated analytics are retained per Google Firebase's default retention settings.

7. Your rights

You can clear app data or uninstall the App at any time to stop all data processing. You may contact us to request deletion of any diagnostic data linked to your device identifier.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
